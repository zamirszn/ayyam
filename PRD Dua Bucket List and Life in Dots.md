# PRD: Ayyam — Dua Bucket List & Life in Dots

**Platforms:** Android, Web (PWA) | **Framework:** Flutter | **AI:** Google Gemini (user-supplied key) | **UI:** Nocturne UI | **Status:** Draft v1

## 1. Overview

Ayyam (أيام, "days") is a calm, private companion app with two features:

1. **Dua Bucket List.** Every morning (default 6:00 AM, customizable) the user is notified with a *new* Islamic supplication they have never been shown before. They read it, tick it as recited, and it is permanently added to their lifetime collection. The goal is to accumulate as many authentic supplications as possible over a lifetime.
2. **Life in Dots.** A visualization of a 100-year life as tiny dots (one per day), overlaid with the user's salat record: completed days, missed days, and make-up (repaid) days.

Each feature can be switched off independently. Onboarding is short, warm and simple.

## 2. Goals and Non-Goals

**Goals**

- Build a daily habit of learning and reciting one new dua.
- Guarantee no dua is ever repeated.
- Work reliably offline via prefetching.
- Show every dua with Arabic, transliteration, translation, benefits and verifiable sources.
- Give a sobering, motivating picture of life in days and salat consistency.
- Keep all user data on-device; the user controls their own AI key.

**Non-Goals (v1)**

- iOS, desktop, or social/sharing features.
- User accounts, cloud sync or a backend server.
- Issuing religious rulings (fatwa). The app is a tracker and learning aid.
- Audio recitation (candidate for v2).

## 3. Target Users

Muslims who want a gentle daily dhikr/dua habit and an honest view of their prayer record; comfortable creating a free Gemini API key. Primary persona: a young adult who wants consistency without noise.

## 4. Onboarding Flow

Goal: under 90 seconds, 5 screens, skippable where possible.

1. **Welcome.** One sentence of purpose, a single "Begin" button.
2. **Choose features.** Two toggle cards: *Dua Bucket List* and *Life in Dots*. At least one must stay on. Changeable later in Settings.
3. **Dua setup** (if enabled): pick notification time (default 6:00 AM); paste Gemini API key with a short "how to get a key" link; "Test key" button; choose translation language (default English).
4. **Life in Dots setup** (if enabled): enter date of birth; optionally enter how many years of prayers were missed or unobserved in the past (see 6.4) to seed make-up debt.
5. **Permissions.** Explain and request: notifications, exact alarms (Android 12+), and battery optimization exemption, each with a one-line reason. Then land on Today.

## 5. Feature A: Dua Bucket List

### 5.1 User Stories

- As a user, I get a notification at my chosen time each day with that day's new dua.
- As a user, I open it and see Arabic, transliteration, translation, benefits and sources.
- As a user, I mark it as recited and the day is completed.
- As a user, I can browse previous days and my full collection.
- As a user, I keep a streak and can see my total count.
- As a user, I still get tomorrow's dua when I have no internet.

### 5.2 Dua Card (content model)

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| id | Stable hash of the normalized Arabic text (diacritics and whitespace stripped) |
| arabic | Fully voweled Arabic text |
| transliteration | Latin script |
| translation | In user's chosen language |
| benefits | Virtue or context for the dua, only as stated in the source |
| occasion | Optional (morning, travel, distress, etc.) |
| sources | One or more: Quran surah:ayah, or hadith collection + number + grading (e.g. Sahih / Hasan) |
| dateAssigned | The day it was scheduled for |
| status | scheduled, shown, recited |
| recitedAt | Timestamp when ticked |

### 5.3 Functional Requirements

**Generation**

- FR-A1: Each day's dua is generated by Gemini using a structured-output (JSON schema) prompt.
- FR-A2: The prompt includes the list of already-collected duas (Arabic first words or IDs) and instructs the model not to repeat them.
- FR-A3: **Non-repetition is enforced in code, not just the prompt.** After generation, compute the ID hash and compare against the local store. On a duplicate, retry up to 3 times with a stronger exclusion list, then fall back to a different category prompt.
- FR-A4: Every dua must include at least one verifiable source. Responses without sources are rejected and regenerated.
- FR-A5: Duas must come only from the Quran and authentic hadith (Sahih or Hasan). The prompt requires grading; the UI displays it.

**Prefetch and offline**

- FR-A6: Maintain a rolling buffer of **5 future days** of duas. Whenever the app or background worker runs with connectivity, top the buffer back up to 5.
- FR-A7: Notifications are scheduled locally from the stored buffer, so they fire with no internet.
- FR-A8: If the buffer is empty and offline, show a friendly state and retry when connectivity returns.

**Notifications and background**

- FR-A9: Daily local notification at the user-chosen time (default 06:00), changeable in Settings; optional day-of-week exclusions in v1.1.
- FR-A10: **Android:** exact alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`), re-registered on device reboot via a boot receiver, plus WorkManager periodic job for prefetching and buffer top-up. Guide the user to disable battery optimization for the app.
- FR-A11: Tapping the notification opens that day's dua.
- FR-A12: Notification body shows the translation preview only; the full card is in-app.

**Recitation and streaks**

- FR-A13: A "Mark as recited" button completes the day. It is only enabled for the current day (and not for future days).
- FR-A14: Streak = consecutive days completed. Show current streak, longest streak and total duas collected.
- FR-A15: A missed day breaks the streak. The missed dua stays in the history as "not recited" and can be recited later without restoring the streak (optional "grace day" setting in v1.1).

**History and collection**

- FR-A16: Swipe or calendar navigation to previous days.
- FR-A17: A Collection screen lists all recited duas with search and filters (occasion, source type).
- FR-A18: Sources are tappable and show full reference details.

### 5.4 Trust and Accuracy Safeguards

AI can fabricate duas or misattribute hadith. This is the single biggest product risk, so:

- Always display the source and its grading prominently.
- Show a short notice: "Generated with AI. Please verify with a qualified scholar or trusted source."
- Add a "Report an issue" action that flags a dua locally and hides it from future recitation prompts.
- Optional v1.1: a second Gemini pass that verifies the reference exists and that the Arabic matches it.
- Consider bundling a small curated seed list of verified duas as a fallback when AI output fails validation.

## 6. Feature B: Life in Dots

### 6.1 Concept

A 100-year life shown as dots: **36,500 days**, one dot per day (leap days approximated or included precisely from DOB). Dots are grouped in rows of one year (about 365 dots) so a user can see 100 rows, or zoomed to decades.

### 6.2 Dot States

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Lived, completed | All 5 daily prayers prayed |
| Lived, missed | One or more prayers missed that day |
| Lived, repaid | Missed day later made up |
| Unrecorded | Lived days before the app was installed with no data (shown neutral, or seeded by the user's estimate) |
| Today | Highlighted |
| Remaining | Future days, faint |

### 6.3 Functional Requirements

- FR-B1: User enters date of birth in onboarding; editable in Settings.
- FR-B2: Render with a custom painter (Flutter `CustomPainter`/canvas) for performance; pinch to zoom, tap a dot to see the date and status.
- FR-B3: Daily check-in (also available from a reminder, off by default): mark today as *all 5 prayed* or *missed*.
- FR-B4: Counters: days lived, days remaining (to age 100), percent of life lived.
- FR-B5: Backfill: user can set a past range (e.g. from age of maturity) as complete, missed, or estimated.

### 6.4 Make-up (Repayment) Mechanic

- FR-B6: Each missed day creates **one make-up debt**. Repaying a day means completing **5 nafl prayers**, which the user ticks off with a single "Repaid" button on that day (or a 5-step tap counter).
- FR-B7: A "Make-up" screen lists outstanding debts, oldest first, with a counter such as "3 of 5 nafilas done" and a one-tap complete action.
- FR-B8: A repaid dot changes from the missed color to the repaid color.
- FR-B9: Past-years seeding: if the user says they missed prayers for N years, create the corresponding debt count. *The exact formula is an open question (see section 11).*

### 6.5 Analytics

- Total years of prayer missed (outstanding), total years completed, total repaid.
- Life in years: lived vs remaining.
- Completion rate (last 30 days, last year, lifetime).
- Projected time to clear debt at the current repayment pace (e.g. "At 2 days per week, 4.2 years").
- Simple charts: monthly stacked bar (completed / missed / repaid) and a debt burn-down line.

## 7. Settings

- Feature toggles: Dua Bucket List on/off, Life in Dots on/off. Turning a feature off hides its tabs and cancels its notifications, but **does not delete data**.
- Notification time and permissions status.
- Gemini key: view masked, replace, remove, test.
- Translation language.
- Export and import data (JSON), delete all data.

## 8. UI and Design

- Use the **Nocturne UI** skill/design system (github.com/zamirszn/nocturne-ui) as the base for tokens, typography, components and  aesthetic. Confirm exact component names and tokens when implementing.
- Screens: Onboarding, Today (dua card), History/Calendar, Collection, Life in Dots, Make-up, Analytics, Settings.

## 9. Technical Approach

**Stack:** Flutter (Android + Web). Suggested packages: `flutter_local_notifications`, `workmanager`, `flutter_secure_storage`, `drift` (SQLite; IndexedDB on web), `riverpod`, `google_generative_ai` or direct Gemini REST.

**Data (local-first):** tables for `duas`, `dua_schedule`, `prayer_days`, `makeup_progress`, `settings`. No server.

**API key security**

- Android: store in Keystore-backed `flutter_secure_storage`.
- Web: browsers have no truly secure storage. Options: keep the key in memory only per session, or encrypt it at rest with a user passphrase using WebCrypto. Be transparent about this limitation in the UI.
- Never log the key; send only over HTTPS directly to Google. Advise the user to restrict the key in Google AI Studio.

**Web platform limits.** A browser cannot run a guaranteed always-on background service. Web delivers notifications via a PWA service worker with Web Push, which requires a push backend or has limited reliability, and iOS-style constraints may apply. For v1, treat web as best-effort: in-app daily dua on open, optional notifications where the browser supports them. Android is the reliable reminder platform.

**Android background.** Exact alarms, boot receiver, WorkManager for top-up fetches, and a battery-optimization exemption prompt. A persistent foreground service is not recommended (high battery cost and Play Store scrutiny); alarms plus WorkManager achieve the same reliability.

**Cost and rate limits.** One Gemini call per day plus top-up calls is cheap; handle 429 and key-invalid errors gracefully.

## 10. Success Metrics

- Day-7 and day-30 retention.
- Percentage of notified days where the dua is marked recited.
- Average streak length.
- Onboarding completion rate and time-to-first-dua.
- Duplicate dua rate (target: 0).
- Rate of user-reported inaccurate duas (target: <1%).
- Percent of notifications delivered on time on Android.

## 11. Open Questions

1. **"3x days in that year" for past prayers.** Your note said if a user has missed prayers for 1 year, it should be "3x days in that year." Please clarify: is it 3 times the number of days, a specific number of nafilas, or something else? This changes the seeding formula in FR-B9.
2. **Religious framing of the nafl make-up.** The 5-nafilas repayment is a personal devotional mechanic. Many scholars hold that missed obligatory prayers must be made up with the same prayers (qada), and that nafl does not replace them. Recommend wording the feature as a *personal make-up practice*, and optionally allowing a configurable repayment (e.g. 5 nafilas or qada of the missed prayers) plus a note to consult a scholar.
3. **Per-prayer tracking.** Track only "all 5 prayed or not", or log each of the five individually? Per-prayer gives richer analytics but adds daily friction.
4. **Web notifications scope** for v1 given browser limitations (see section 9).
5. **Lifespan.** Keep 100 years fixed, or allow a custom target age?
6. **Seed content.** Ship a small verified dua dataset as a fallback or rely only on Gemini?
